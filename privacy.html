Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 21, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Veeru Student App ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you register for an account, we collect:

  • Name: Your full name for account identification
  • Email Address: For account verification and communication
  • Mobile Number: For account security and login purposes
  • School Name: To personalize your learning experience
  • Class/Grade: To provide appropriate educational content
  • Board Type: (CBSE, ICSE, State Board, Scholarship) for content customization

Usage Information

We automatically collect:

  • Learning Progress: Quiz scores, completed chapters, and study statistics
  • App Activity: Features used, time spent on different sections
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version (for app optimization)
  • Log Data: App crashes, errors, and performance metrics

Content You Create

  • Quiz answers and scores
  • Bookmarked content
  • Language preferences
  • Downloaded offline content

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide Services: Deliver educational content, track progress, and personalize learning
  • Account Management: Create and manage your user account
  • Improve App: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features and fix bugs
  • Communication: Send important updates about the app (no marketing emails)
  • Support: Respond to your questions and provide technical assistance
  • Security: Protect against unauthorized access and fraudulent activity

Data Storage and Security

Security Measures

  • All data transmission is encrypted using HTTPS/SSL
  • Passwords are securely hashed and never stored in plain text
  • We use secure servers hosted on Railway (https://railway.app)
  • Regular security audits and updates

Data Retention

  • Account data is retained as long as your account is active
  • You can request account deletion at any time
  • Learning progress data is kept to track your educational journey
  • Deleted accounts are permanently removed within 30 days

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We DO NOT:

  • ❌ Sell your personal information to third parties
  • ❌ Share your data with advertisers
  • ❌ Use your information for marketing purposes
  • ❌ Share student data with educational institutions without consent

We MAY Share Information:

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize us to share data
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or government request
  • Service Providers: With trusted partners who help us operate the app (e.g., hosting providers), under strict confidentiality agreements
  • Safety: To protect the rights, property, or safety of our users

Children's Privacy

Veeru Student App is designed for students, including children under 13. We comply with applicable children's privacy laws:

  • We collect only necessary information for educational purposes
  • Parental consent may be required for users under 13
  • We do not knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children
  • Parents can request to review, update, or delete their child's information

Parents/Guardians: If you believe your child has provided personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately.

Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Update: Modify your account information at any time
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt-Out: Disable certain data collection features
  • Export: Download your learning progress and data

Third-Party Services

Our app may contain links to third-party content (e.g., YouTube videos, Google Drive documents). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external services. Please review their privacy policies separately.

Offline Content

Content downloaded for offline use is stored locally on your device and is not accessible to us unless you sync your progress online.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by:

  • Posting the new policy in the app
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Sending an email notification (for major changes)

Your continued use of the app after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

International Users

Our servers are located globally. By using the app, you consent to the transfer and processing of your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.
